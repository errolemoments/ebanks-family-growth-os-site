The Ebanks Family Growth OS

A private budgeting desk for our family. It runs on our own computers and works with YNAB, our Google Sheet and our bank, so we can cover what’s due, see what’s coming, and grow our savings back.

Paycheck plan

Split each paycheck the way our Google Sheet does, see what still needs covering, then send it to YNAB.

Week by week

What’s due this week, the weeks ahead, and whether we need to dip into savings before payday.

Month plan

What to talk about together each month, next month drafted, once-a-year costs and celebrations.

Savings and Medical

Grow back the Emergency Fund and Paycheck Protection, and keep a plan for medical bills.

Sign in with Google

Signing in with Google is optional. If you choose it, Growth OS keeps an encrypted copy of your budget in your own Google Drive, so your computers stay in step and a new computer can pick up where you left off. It can also keep a Google Sheets copy of your transactions and plans.

Growth OS only gets access to the files it creates in your Drive. It can’t see, read or change anything else in your Google Drive. Your budget file is encrypted with a password only you know, so not even Google can read it.

Read exactly what Growth OS does with your information in the privacy policy.

Who it’s for

Growth OS is a personal app made for the Ebanks family and the people we share our budget with. It isn’t sold, it has no ads, and it doesn’t collect anything about you for us.
